Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Jubilant Breeze ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our applications.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Name, email address, and profile details
  • Authentication data when signing in via Apple or Google

1.2 User Content

  • Images you upload (including jewelry, product, or model photos)
  • Content may include human faces

1.3 Face Data (Important Disclosure)

Some features process images containing human faces to generate AI-based outputs.

We explicitly confirm:

  • We do NOT perform facial recognition
  • We do NOT identify individuals
  • We do NOT create or store biometric identifiers
  • Face data is used only for image processing features

1.4 Usage Data

  • App activity, feature usage, and interactions
  • Device information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Anonymized analytics

1.5 Payment Information

All purchases are processed by Apple via In-App Purchases. We do not collect, access, or store payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide AI-powered features (image generation, editing, try-on)
  • Operate and maintain app functionality
  • Manage accounts, credits, and transactions
  • Improve performance and AI accuracy
  • Send service-related communications
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal data.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Your uploaded content may be processed using AI systems, including trusted third-party providers.

  • Processing occurs only to deliver requested results
  • Data is transmitted securely
  • Data is not used to train AI models without consent

3.1 Third-Party Processing

We use service providers for AI processing, hosting, and analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to:

  • Process data only on our instructions
  • Maintain confidentiality and security
  • Not use data for their own purposes

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited data with:

  • Cloud storage providers
  • AI processing services
  • Analytics providers

We do not share data for advertising using your uploaded content or face data.

5. Data Retention

  • Uploaded images and generated outputs are stored for up to 30 days
  • After 30 days, data is permanently deleted
  • Account data is retained while active
  • Deleted accounts are erased within 30 days

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

6.1 GDPR Rights (EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

6.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is collected
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of sale of personal data (we do not sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise any rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage with restricted access
  • Regular security monitoring

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
